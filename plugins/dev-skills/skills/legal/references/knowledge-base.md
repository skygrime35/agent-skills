# Legal knowledge base — obligation matrix (French/EU law, web projects)

file_reviewed_at: 2026-09  # RGPD-5, FISC-1, JEUX-1 refreshed 2026-09-01 (evabet audit); COOK-1/2 note: délib. 2020-091/092 amended by délib. 2025-131 (multi-device consent) and reco pixels de suivi 12/03/2026, not replaced

## 0. How to read this matrix

Entries are looked up by **trigger** (a detected feature), never browsed for inspiration — the minimal-set doctrine forbids applying an entry whose trigger is absent. Each entry: trigger · basis (exact articles) · requires (the minimum) · severity-if-missing · minimal implementation · volatility (`stable` | `moving(<why>)`) · `reviewed_at`.

**Staleness / web-research protocol.** A web check is REQUIRED before relying on an entry when: (1) `volatility: moving`; (2) `reviewed_at` > 6 months old; (3) the point is project-specific (a médiateur's current tariff, a mark's INPI status, a provider's DPF listing). Trusted sources, in order: legifrance.gouv.fr (« version en vigueur »!), cnil.fr, bofip.impots.gouv.fr, economie.gouv.fr/DGCCRF, service-public.fr, eur-lex.europa.eu, arcom.fr; secondary sources only to locate primaries. Recording a refresh: bump the entry's `reviewed_at`, update the facts, keep a `sources:` line (URL + consultation date). If a refresh CHANGES a rule, scan generated corpora for clauses citing the old rule ⇒ P2 « texte publié obsolète » findings.

**Out of scope** (referral rule — emit ASK-A-PROFESSIONAL naming the professional and the precise question; never generate): contrats de travail/droit social; statuts, PV, gouvernance d'association (flag that a PV is needed, never draft it); levées de fonds; optimisation fiscale (only mentions/thresholds are in scope); droit non-UE; contentieux; assurance; AIPD complète chiffrée; dépôts de marque et accords de coexistence.

## 2. IDENT — publisher identification

**IDENT-1 Mentions légales.** trigger: any site reachable in France. basis: LCEN (loi 2004-575) — since loi SREN n° 2024-449 (23/05/2024) the identification duties moved from old art. 6-III to **art. 1-1**; sanctions at **art. 1-2** (1 an, 75 000 € PP / 375 000 € PM). requires (personne morale): dénomination + forme, siège, SIRET/SIREN, **téléphone**, email, directeur de la publication, hébergeur (nom, adresse, téléphone). severity: page absent = P0; a missing field = P1 (phone-only omission: DGCCRF enforcement rare ⇒ eligible for accepted_risks, reported as such). minimal: one ~15-line page (template T1). volatility: stable. sources: legifrance JORFTEXT000049563368 (2026-08-09); numbering corroborated by specialist counsel — cite « article 1-1 de la LCEN » but re-verify the consolidated text before quoting numbers in a published page. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**IDENT-2 B2C extras.** trigger: B2C selling. basis: C. conso L112-1 (prix TTC affichés), L616-1 (médiateur), CGI franchise mention. requires: TTC prices everywhere, TVA mention (FISC-1), médiateur identity (CONSO-6) in mentions + CGV. severity: P1. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

## 3. CGU / CONSO

**CGU-1 CGU essentials.** trigger: user accounts. basis: droit commun + DSA art. 14 (T&C must state moderation policy clearly, child-readable if minors). requires: objet/acceptation versionnée, âge minimum, règles d'usage, (UGC ⇒ licence + modération), suspension motivée, responsabilité non abusive (R212-1: blanket exclusions vs consumers = clauses noires), évolution avec re-acceptation. severity: no CGU with accounts = P1. minimal: template T2. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**CONSO-1 Pre-contractual info.** trigger: B2C distance contract. basis: L111-1, L221-5 C. conso. requires: characteristics, price TTC, duration/renewal, withdrawal conditions — in the CGV before order. severity: P1 (withdrawal omission ⇒ délai extended to 12 months, L221-20). volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**CONSO-2 Withdrawal 14 j.** trigger: B2C distance contract. basis: L221-18 (14 j), **L221-25** (service started early at express request ⇒ withdrawal keeps working, pay prorata of what ran), L221-28 13° (one-shot digital content: full waiver with express consent + acknowledgment), model form = **annexe à R221-1, version 28/05/2022** (décret 2022-424). **Online « bouton de rétractation »**: ordonnance n° 2026-2 du 5 janvier 2026 (intitulé officiel « relative à la commercialisation à distance de services financiers auprès des consommateurs », transposition dir. 2023/2673) art. 3, modifiant **L221-21 C. conso**, + décret n° 2026-3 (art. D.221-5) — en vigueur depuis le 19/06/2026; ne pas citer un intitulé « relative à la fonction de rétractation en ligne », qui n'est pas l'intitulé officiel du texte. requires: CGV clause (14 j, how — email suffices, form reproduced/linked); SaaS subscription ⇒ prefer the **L221-25 prorata route** (friendlier, and the refund path must exist in code — META-1); when a « bouton de rétractation » is claimed, cite L221-21 + décret 2026-3, not an invented ordonnance title. severity: nothing at all = P0 pre-sales; missing form = P1; wrong ordonnance title in published text = P2 (texte publié obsolète/inexact). volatility: moving(texte récent, contrôler la version consolidée). sources: legifrance.gouv.fr JORFTEXT000053298845 (2026-08-11). reviewed_at: 2026-08

**CONSO-3 Order button + pre-ticked ban.** trigger: online checkout. basis: **L221-14** (« commande avec obligation de paiement » — courts judge the button text alone); **L121-17** (consentement exprès for any supplementary payment; pre-ticked ⇒ refund), sanction L121-18 (3 000 € PP / 15 000 € PM). severity: P1. volatility: stable. sources: legifrance LEGIARTI000032226854, LEGIARTI000032227250 (2026-08-09). reviewed_at: 2026-08

**CONSO-4 Renewal + cancellation.** trigger: auto-renewing consumer subscription. basis: **L215-1 applies only to fixed-term contracts with tacit renewal** (notice 3→1 months before deadline) — a month-to-month at-will subscription has no fixed term ⇒ Chatel notice inapplicable (literal reading, corroborated doctrine; no dedicated DGCCRF fiche found). **L215-1-1 + D215-1..3** (décret 2023-417): « résiliation en 3 clics » free online path, mandatory since 01/06/2023 for ALL electronically concluded contracts, at-will included. requires: the 3-click feature + a CGV clause matching the real flow; Chatel clause only when a commitment period exists. severity: 3-click path absent = P1; Chatel missing when triggered = P1. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**CONSO-5 Trial→paid disclosure.** trigger: free trial converting to paid. basis: L221-5/L221-14 info duties + DGCCRF dark-pattern doctrine. requires: conversion date + amount disclosed BEFORE card entry; reminder email; cancellation during trial truly free. severity: P1. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**CONSO-6 Médiateur de la consommation.** trigger: any professional (association included) selling to consumers. basis: L612-1 (right), **L616-1** (publish médiateur's name, postal address, website — site, CGV, bons de commande), R616-1; sanction L641-1 up to 3 000 € PP / 15 000 € PM. requires: a **signed convention BEFORE concluding B2C contracts**; announcing an unsigned médiateur is worse than a placeholder — the certain basis is L616-1/L641-1 (DGCCRF administrative fine); an additional **L121-2 pratique-trompeuse qualification is plausible but NOT settled** by any published precedent or official doctrine (researched 2026-08-16) — present it as a possible aggravation, never as acquired. **No documented tolerance for a transitional "médiateur en cours de désignation" placeholder either**: the obligation is presented as immediate from the first B2C sale (economie.gouv.fr, 2026-08-16) — an honest placeholder mitigates but is not a compliant regime. minimal for micro-structures: **CM2C ~48 € TTC / 3 ans** (≤10 salariés; +36 €/saisine; cm2c.net, tél 01 89 47 00 14, SIREN 831 213 871; address **49 rue de Ponthieu 75008 Paris** — CM2C moved, the former 14 rue Saint Jean 75017 is invalid for new adhesions) or AME Conso ~180 € HT / 3 ans — re-verify tariff and CECMC referencing at adhesion time (official CECMC PDF returned 403 on 2026-08-16). **The EU ODR platform closed 2025-07-20 (Reg. (UE) 2024/3228): REMOVE any ODR mention/link from CGV** — only the national médiateur remains. severity: P1 (P0 if texts falsely announce an unsigned médiateur when payments open). volatility: moving(médiateur tariffs/list). sources: cm2c.net/tarifs.php, capeb09.fr (CM2C address change), legifrance L641-1 LEGIARTI000032224624, eur-lex 2024/3228, legifrance L616-1 (2026-08-16). reviewed_at: 2026-08

**CONSO-7 Garantie de conformité numérique.** trigger: paid B2C digital service/content. basis: **L224-25-1 à L224-25-32** (ordonnance 2021-1247, in force 01/01/2022) + **décret n° 2022-946** (since 01/10/2022): a mandatory **encadré** summarizing the guarantee in the CGV, model wording in the regulatory annex (numbering of the annex articles to re-verify on Légifrance when drafting). severity: encadré missing = P1. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**CONSO-8 Invoices.** trigger: B2C sales by the franchised association. basis: general invoicing rules + franchise mention. requires: seller identity + SIREN, buyer, number, date, designation, price HT-equivalent with the franchise mention instead of VAT; B2C invoice mandatory ≥ 25 € TTC or on request. severity: P2. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

## 4. RGPD

**RGPD-1 Privacy policy.** trigger: any personal data. basis: art. 12-14 RGPD. requires: identity of controller, contact (DPO or generic), one table finalité × base légale × données × durée (generated from the art. 30 register — RGPD-02 keeps them in 2-way sync), recipients = the REAL processor list, transfer + safeguard per non-EU processor (DPF/SCC), rights incl. « saisir la CNIL » + URL, art. 22 statement if relevant. severity: absent = P0 with accounts; silent on an actual processing (e.g. AI) = P1. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**RGPD-2 Registre art. 30.** trigger: non-occasional processing (any SaaS). basis: art. 30 RGPD — the <250-employee exemption does not cover regular processing, so in practice required. requires: one entry per processing + processor table with DPA status. Placeholders in the registre while paid prod runs = P0. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**RGPD-3 AIPD/DPIA.** trigger: 2+ EDPB criteria (WP248rev01): vulnerable persons (minors), innovative use (AI), sensitive/highly personal data (filmed people, voice), large scale… basis: art. 35 RGPD + CNIL list délib. 2018-327. requires: AIPD, or at minimum a **documented screening decision** (accountability art. 5-2). The full quantified AIPD is ASK-A-PROFESSIONAL territory; the skill generates the screening only. severity: minors + AI with neither = P1. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**RGPD-4 Consent proof.** trigger: consent-based processing / contract acceptance. basis: art. 7 RGPD. requires: versioned, timestamped, tamper-evident records (append-only table or equivalent); version constants sync'd with published texts (V1). severity: P2. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**RGPD-5 Minors.** trigger: users 15–17 (or younger). basis: art. 8 RGPD + **art. 45 LIL: 15 ans** = autonomous consent for information-society services; below 15 ⇒ joint parent+minor consent. Note: loi 2023-566 « majorité numérique » **never entered into force** (no decrees, EU-conformity condition unmet); the successor law (réseaux sociaux <15, adopted 21/07/2026) was **censured by the Conseil constitutionnel on 14/08/2026** (disproportionate restriction of minors' freedom of expression) and **never entered into force**; a rewrite is announced for spring 2027 — nothing applicable today beyond art. 45 LIL. **Multilingual sites**: art. 8 RGPD default is 16; Germany did not lower it — a 15-year-old resident in Germany needs parental consent (threshold follows the minor's habitual residence, majority EDPB reading — verify before drafting). requires today: age floor enforced at signup matching the CGU claim; plain-language info (CNIL 2021 minors recommendations); no profiling/marketing aimed at minors. severity: no age floor = P1. volatility: moving(rewrite announced 2027). sources: france24.com 2026-08-14 (censure CC); legifrance LEGIARTI000037823135 (art. 45 LIL); cnil.fr recommandation 4 mineurs (2026-09-01). reviewed_at: 2026-09

**RGPD-6 Retention.** trigger: accounts/logs/billing. basis: minimization + specific texts. Reference durations: connection/content-creation logs **1 an** (décret n° 2021-1362, verified still in force 2026-08; identité civile 5 ans post-contract), billing **10 ans** (L123-22 C. com.), prospects 3 ans after last contact, consent/contract proof 5 ans post-relation (prescription), audience measurement raw data ≤ 25 mois, account data anonymized at closure + grace period. requires: each declared duration enforced by an actual purge job (META-1). severity: declared-but-unenforced = P1. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**RGPD-7 AI processing disclosure.** trigger: user content sent to a third-party AI (Gemini, OpenAI…). basis: art. 13/14 + art. 28 (processor) + transfer rules (US provider ⇒ DPF/SCC). requires: the privacy policy names the provider, what is sent, the legal basis (explicit consent gate is the clean minimal), the transfer safeguard; a consent flag + version if consent-based. **Gemini API free tier**: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms sets a default « Unpaid Services » regime (content used to improve products, possible human review, no DPA) — BUT a regional clause makes the « Paid Services » terms (no training use, DPA applies) apply to **all** services, including the free quota, for users in the **EEE/Suisse/Royaume-Uni**. Before asserting a DPA/no-training regime in a published policy, verify the API account/key's EU attachment — an EU-attached account gets paid-tier protections even on the free quota; a non-EU-attached account does not. requires when unverified: flag as an open question, not an assumed protection. severity: policy silent on active AI = P1; asserting a DPA without verifying account attachment = P1 (information trompeuse). volatility: moving(provider ToS). sources: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms (2026-08-11). reviewed_at: 2026-08

## 5. COOK — cookies & trackers

**COOK-1 No-banner regime.** trigger: only strictly necessary cookies. basis: art. 82 LIL; CNIL délib. **2020-091/2020-092** (still the governing doctrine — verified unchanged 2026-08). Exempt: auth/session, cart, load-balancing, consent storage, strict security; audience measurement ONLY under the CNIL exemption config (sole-publisher purpose, no cross-site, no data sale, cookie ≤ 13 mois, raw data ≤ 25 mois, IP anonymized, opt-out offered — a self-hosted Matomo qualifies only so configured). requires: no banner, but a cookies info section listing each cookie/purpose/duration stays due. **reCAPTCHA is NOT exempt** (CNIL constant position since MED-2020-015): enabling it ⇒ prior consent ⇒ banner — the regime-breaking one-flag trap; keep it as a dormant-integration check. severity: non-exempt cookie without consent = P1 (the CNIL's most-fined topic); info section missing = P2. volatility: moving(CNIL cookie doctrine). reviewed_at: 2026-08

**COOK-2 Consent regime.** trigger: any non-essential tracker. basis: art. 82 LIL + délib. 2020-091/092. requires: banner with equal-prominence accept/refuse, no consent wall, choice re-openable, consent stored ≤ 13 mois, cookie table page; « en continuant vous acceptez » is invalid. severity: P1. volatility: moving. reviewed_at: 2026-08

## 6. DSA / hosting

**DSA-1 Hosting baseline.** trigger: hosts UGC. basis: Reg. (UE) 2022/2065 — **art. 11** (contact point authorities), **12** (contact point recipients), **14** (T&C state moderation clearly; child-appropriate if minors), **16** (electronic notice & action: explanation, exact URL, notifier identity+email, good-faith statement), **17** (statement of reasons + redress). Micro/small enterprises (Rec. 2003/361/CE) are exempt from **all of Section 3** (art. 19-28, incl. transparency reports art. 15§2 and the online-platform obligations arts 20–28) via **art. 19**, with the sole exception of **art. 24§3** — which is a duty to communicate, on the coordinator's request, the average monthly number of active EU recipients (feeds the VLOP-threshold computation), **not** a public advertising-transparency obligation. Concretely: **art. 26 (ad labelling) is exempted for micro-enterprises** — ad labelling on a micro-platform's encarts falls back to national law (loyauté C. conso L121-3 s., LCEN art. 20), not the DSA. But **arts 11/12/14/16/17 apply to everyone**, associations included (verified 2026-08). FR coordinator: ARCOM. requires: a report form/mailto capturing art. 16 fields (reachable without account), an art. 17 statement template, contact points published; do not claim a DSA ad-marking duty for an exempt micro-structure. severity: no reporting channel with UGC = P1; no art. 17 statements = P2. volatility: moving(Commission guidance stream). sources: règlement (UE) 2022/2065, texte consolidé (2026-08-11). reviewed_at: 2026-08

**DSA-2 LCEN takedown.** trigger: hosts UGC. basis: LCEN art. 6 (rewritten by SREN to align with DSA): prompt removal upon actual knowledge of manifestly illicit content; an art. 16 DSA notice = presumption of knowledge. requires: an internal handling path with response times; log of decisions. severity: P2 (P1 if notices are demonstrably ignored). volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

## 7. IP

**IP-1 Trademark clearance.** trigger: any product name. basis: CPI L713-2 s. requires: an INPI availability check documented; a known conflict (e.g. a third-party mark in overlapping classes) = tracked decision, ASK-A-PROFESSIONAL (conseil en propriété industrielle) for coexistence/risk. severity: P2 (decision debt, not a drafting task). volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**IP-2 UGC license + image rights.** trigger: users upload content. basis: CPI L131-3 (cession interpretation strict) + art. 9 C. civ. (image). requires: CGU clause — non-exclusive license, purposes limited to operating the service (hosting, display, technical adaptation), duration = account life; uploader warrants third-party rights incl. filmed persons' image rights. Never a blanket « tous droits cédés ». severity: P2. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

## 8. JEUX

**JEUX-1 Règlement de jeu-concours.** trigger: prize draws with real prizes. basis: **L121-20 C. conso** (loteries publicitaires — prohibited only when unfair under L121-1; transposition dir. 2005/29/CE). **L121-36 à L121-41 were ABROGATED on 01/07/2016** (ordonnance 2016-301, recodification) — never cite them as in force, nor their 15 000 €/75 000 € sanctions; the applicable sanctions are those of unfair/misleading practices (L121-2/L121-3, L132-2). **No codified duty to write a règlement** for a free draw — it is a strongly recommended practice (proof of loyalty, opposability of conditions, L121-3 omission risk), not a formal obligation; present it as such. **Huissier deposit + cost-reimbursement obligations abolished by loi n° 2014-1545** (not reintroduced — deposit is optional evidence-keeping). requires (minimum règlement): organizer identity; dates; free-entry conditions (age, territory, one entry/person); prize list + unit value TTC, no cash equivalent; selection method + date; delivery + forfeiture delay; data clause; modification/cancellation for cause; règlement URL; applicable law. Minors: no codified parental-authorization duty for free entry — standard contractual prudence is to require legal-guardian consent **at prize delivery** (present it as practice, not as statutory obligation). severity: real-prize draws with no règlement = P1 (on the L121-3 omission / co-organiser liability ground, not on a formal duty). minimal: one règlement-cadre + per-draw annex (T6). volatility: stable. sources: legifrance LEGIARTI000029947687 (L121-36, abrogé), LEGISCTA000032221007 (section L121-20), wan-avocats.com (fin dépôt huissier) (2026-09-01). reviewed_at: 2026-09

**JEUX-2 Gambling boundary.** trigger: predictions/points/draws of any kind. basis: **L320-1 CSI** (old L322-1 abrogated by ordonnance 2019-1015): prohibition requires sacrifice financier + espérance de gain + hasard — **free entry kills the sacrifice criterion**. requires: keep entry genuinely free (no payment improving winning odds), no cash-out of points, and no gambling vocabulary in the product/marketing (wording.md trap list) — the qualification depends on all three. severity: a paid mechanic touching odds = P0 (activité illégale de jeux d'argent). volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

## 9. FISC

**FISC-1 TVA franchise.** trigger: the entity invoices under the franchise. basis: **art. 293 B CGI**, version in force 01/03/2025 → 01/01/2027: the LF2025 single 25 000 € threshold was suspended then **abrogated** (loi n° 2025-1044 du 03/11/2025); 2026 thresholds: **37 500 € / 41 250 €** (services) and **85 000 € / 93 500 €** (goods/hébergement). Invoice mention « TVA non applicable, art. 293 B du CGI » **remains exact until 31/12/2026**: the CIBS recodification (ordonnance n° 2025-1247, initially 01/09/2026) was **postponed to 01/01/2027 by ordonnance n° 2026-671 du 27/07/2026**, and the article was renumbered **L. 233-3 du CIBS** (not L. 223-3); CGI wording tolerated on invoices until 30/06/2028 (secondary sources). Primary-source verification of ordonnance 2026-671 is partial — re-verify in Q4 2026 before switching the mention; do NOT switch before 01/01/2027. severity: wrong mention = P1; a TVA line while franchised = P1 (CGI art. 283-3: TVA due merely because invoiced). volatility: **moving(recodification postponed — re-check Q4 2026)**. sources: legifrance LEGIARTI000045035275; LEGIARTI000053107172 (art. L223-3 CIBS page citing ordonnance 2026-671); BOI-TVA-DECLA-40-10-10-20260701; JORFTEXT000053091516; kohenavocats.com, synapx.fr (2026-09-01). reviewed_at: 2026-09

**FISC-2 Association non-lucrativité.** trigger: association 1901 with commercial revenue. basis: CGI art. 206; BOFiP **BOI-IS-CHAMP-10-50-10-20** (in force since 2017): gestion désintéressée → concurrence → règle des 4P; franchise des activités lucratives accessoires (206-1 bis): **81 051 € for 2026** (BOI-IS-CHAMP-10-50-20-20, updated 05/08/2026), non-lucrative activity must stay significantly preponderant; beyond ⇒ sectorisation/filialisation (BOI-IS-CHAMP-10-50-20-10) or full requalification. requires from the skill: the correct franchise mention, a threshold watch, and an **ASK-A-PROFESSIONAL flag (expert-comptable associatif)** whenever commercial revenue approaches the threshold or becomes principal — never a structuring recommendation. severity: threshold breach unexamined = P1 flag. volatility: moving(threshold indexed yearly). reviewed_at: 2026-08

## 10. COMM / MISC

**COMM-1 Email marketing.** trigger: marketing emails exist. basis: **L34-5 CPCE**: prior opt-in; soft opt-in exception (existing customer, similar products, easy refusal); unsubscribe in every message. Transactional/lifecycle emails are outside. Note CNIL délib. 2026-042: tracking pixels in emails need consent. severity: P1. volatility: stable. reviewed_at: 2026-08

**MISC-1 Accessibility (EAA).** trigger: B2C digital service/e-commerce. basis: dir. (UE) 2019/882, transposed (loi 2023-171 art. 16, ordonnance 2023-859, décret 2023-931, arrêté 09/10/2023; RGAA as FR technical reference), applicable since **28/06/2025**. **Micro-enterprise exemption: <10 persons AND ≤ 2 M€** — automatic, no declaration; whether a near-zero-employee association is an « undertaking » at all is unsettled (no official doctrine found) ⇒ typical output: an accepted_risks-eligible P3 note, not a generated statement. severity: for exempt micro-structures = P3 informational. volatility: moving(early enforcement doctrine). reviewed_at: 2026-08

**MISC-2 AI Act transparency.** trigger: AI-generated content shown to users. basis: Reg. (UE) 2024/1689 **art. 50, applicable since 02/08/2026**. Machine-readable marking of outputs (50(2)) is the PROVIDER's duty (Google), not the deployer's. Deployer duties (50(4)): disclose deepfakes; disclose AI-generated text **published to inform the public on matters of public interest** (unless human editorial control + responsibility) — user-content summaries inside a private tool are likely outside, but the qualification is unsettled. requires: a visible « généré par IA » label on AI outputs is the cheap, safe minimum; privacy-policy disclosure per RGPD-7. severity: P3 today (P2 if outputs are public-facing informational). volatility: **moving(first-year guidance expected)**. reviewed_at: 2026-08
